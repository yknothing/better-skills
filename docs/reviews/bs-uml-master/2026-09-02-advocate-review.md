## Strongest Aspect

The single best design move of R7 is the insight recorded in research section 七 and then actually built: a validator that must be reached through prose has the bindingness of prose. R3–R6 had accumulated eight mechanical checks (C1–C8) and a fit gate, and the trajectory review shows all of them were bypassed by one unrun command, because the command needed a markdown draft that an HTML delivery never produced. `verify-delivery.js` inverts the cost structure — the compliant path is now the cheapest path: one call takes the page itself, builds the mirror, parses on the reader's pinned renderer (which is where the "blank page" actually lived), renders, fit-checks, contract-checks, citation-checks, and emits a receipt whose lines already satisfy C8, hash-stamped so a typed imitation is one more lie to tell. My replay is the proof that this is not a description of intent: the same artifact that survived eleven revisions with zero findings produced, in 30 seconds, the failing line number, the two illegible diagrams, and the eight fabricated identifiers. That is the repo's core thesis — bind with tools where prose fails — applied one level up, to the entry point rather than the checks.

## One Improvement

Make `check-evidence.js` E3 precise enough to promote. Today the identifier cross-check is line-scoped: every identifier-shaped token on the citing line is tested against every citation on that line, so the Output Contract's own prescription — "every relationship edge's kind claim cited individually", naturally written as one Evidence line — turns an honest seven-edge delivery into seven fabrication-flavoured WARNs (verified above; one citation per line drops it to one legitimate WARN). Because of that noise the signature had to ship as WARN, and a WARN does not gate VERDICT, so the exact failure R7 was built for — `batch_id` cited to `skills.json:50-65` — still passes the verifier once the unrelated parse and fit FAILs are fixed. The fix is two steps, fixture first: (1) scope identifiers per citation segment — split the citing line at `;`, `·`, and list-item boundaries, and attribute each identifier to the nearest citation in its segment, with a fixture of an honest seven-edge single-line Evidence field asserting 0 WARN; (2) once that fixture passes, promote "identifier appears nowhere in the cited file" from WARN to FAIL (keep "elsewhere in file, tighten the line" as WARN), with the usage-sample laundering fixture asserting exit 1. This converts the verifier's biggest remaining blind spot into a gate, and removes the WARN fatigue that would otherwise train the next weak model to ignore the one signal that catches confident fiction.
