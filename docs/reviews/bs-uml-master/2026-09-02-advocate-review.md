## Strongest Aspect

The single best design move of R7 is the insight recorded in research section 七 and then actually built: a validator that must be reached through prose has the bindingness of prose. R3–R6 had accumulated eight mechanical checks (C1–C8) and a fit gate, and the trajectory review shows all of them were bypassed by one unrun command, because the command needed a markdown draft that an HTML delivery never produced. `verify-delivery.js` inverts the cost structure — the compliant path is now the cheapest path: one call takes the page itself, builds the mirror, parses on the reader's pinned renderer (which is where the "blank page" actually lived), renders, fit-checks, contract-checks, citation-checks, and emits a receipt whose lines already satisfy C8, hash-stamped so a typed imitation is one more lie to tell, and — since R7.1 — simulated into every block before the contract check so that the first receipt is already the final receipt. My replays are the proof that this is not a description of intent: the same artifact that survived eleven revisions with zero findings produced, in 30 seconds, the failing line number, the two illegible diagrams, and five fabricated identifiers as hard FAILs. That is the repo's core thesis — bind with tools where prose fails — applied one level up, to the entry point rather than the checks.
