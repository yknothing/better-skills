## Strongest Aspect

The strongest aspect is that openness is implemented as a separate authority
path, not as weaker evidence standards. `evidence-contract.md` explicitly says
missing qualifying demand requires `TEST_FIRST` unless a separately documented
Conviction Thesis passes the Frontier Gate, and it also forbids
`OWNER_ACCEPTED_RISK` from satisfying the Product Gate. The main workflow then
makes the transition explicit: when the Product Gate is blocked but the Frontier
Gate passes, select `CONVICTION_BET`; authorize one bounded build-and-exposure
cycle; keep the `UNVALIDATED_CONVICTION` label; and require a new owner decision
before any repeat. This gives a capable founder room to act before a category has
formed without granting persistence, confidence, or youth an exemption from
reality.
